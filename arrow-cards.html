Trading: other ways to split it

Stack the cards and the smaller cards cover the zeros, but the zeros are still there underneath: 34 is 30 + 4. Tap a card to lift it up and look. The expander shows the same number in hundreds, tens or ones. Trading keeps the total the same: 234 = 200 + 30 + 4 = 200 + 20 + 14.
