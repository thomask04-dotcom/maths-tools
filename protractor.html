Protractor
Practise measuring:
Drag the round ends of the arms to change the angle. Drag the protractor to move it, and drag its orange ⟳ handle to turn it. Put the centre cross on the vertex and line up 0° with one arm, then read the scale. Practise measuring gives a set of angles, each turned a different way with different arm lengths. Try "The same angle, drawn different ways" to show that turning an angle or changing its arm length doesn't change its size.
