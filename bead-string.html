Bead string: tap a bead to move the peg to it, and the beads up to the peg are counted. The beads come in groups of 5 colours, so you can see 5s and 10s without counting by ones. Double bead string: compare two amounts. The shaded part shows how many more. Rekenrek: tap a bead to slide it (and the beads before it) to the left. "Show 8 + 5" shows how to bridge through 10.
